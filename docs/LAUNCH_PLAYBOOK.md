# Hood Rivals — beta launch

**Small world. Unfinished business.** Watch rival captains, learn the frontier, and settle a six-round contest of territory, pacts and betrayal. Free to play. Optional wallet identity. Cosmetics only.

Show a short continuous clip: follow an island captain → enter guided practice → make a tactical decision → finish a real match → show its actual battle report → invite a friend. Label the island local simulation and the match saved tactical play. Use actual outcomes, never fabricated player counts or earnings.

Launch a playable beta before a paid campaign. Invite small cohorts and measure first practice completion, first normal match completion, return play, invitation acceptance and wallet rejection/drop-off. Collect why people quit. Add analytics only with appropriate disclosure and consent; analytics infrastructure is not included.

Lead with rivalry and memorable decisions. Wallet/token information belongs after the player understands the game. Do not imply that increasing users, TVL, token retirement or launchpad listing guarantees a rising price. Sell optional cosmetic identity, not investment performance.

Future shared-agent hosting should follow observed demand and a server-side architecture review. Do not advertise unlimited external agents in this release.
